Origin Story

From Visual Communication to Property Journeys

INITIUM was founded by a team that believed real estate could be different. Not a numbers game. Not a race to the bottom. But a craft — built on seeing people clearly and guiding them with precision and heart.

With a background in art and visual communication, the founder brought a storyteller's eye to an industry that too often forgets the human behind the transaction. Street culture, collectibles, and an obsession with authenticity shaped a brand that doesn't sell — it ignites.

"I want to be remembered as trustworthy, reliable, and the go-to person for work or personal matters."

— Tasso Chan, Founder
