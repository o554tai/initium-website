## 🖼️ FONT SUBSTITUTIONS

| Specified | Substitute |
|-----------|------------|
| Montserrat Bold | Montserrat (Bold weight) |

> Consistency > exact font name. Pick one substitute and stick with it.

## ⚡ SPEED TIPS

1. **Export templates first.** Do Opening Sting + End Card before touching raw footage. Saves 5 min/video.
2. **Duplicate projects.** Edit Video 1 fully, then `File → Duplicate Project` and swap footage.
3. **Auto captions.** `Text → Auto Captions → English`. Fix: Ryley, Rykiel, INITIUM.
4. **Master project.** Save one project with all 3 templates placed but no footage. Duplicate for future videos.
5. **Batch exports.** Queue all 4 exports and let CapCut run overnight.
