Rules

Do's & Don'ts

Do

Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the cap-height of "I". Use the iridescent accent sparingly for highlights, not backgrounds. Place the logo on white, off-white, or brand green only.

Don't

Stretch, rotate, or recolor the logo mark. Use drop shadows or heavy effects on the lockup. Place the logo on busy photography or clashing colors (red, orange, bright purple).

Do

Use Space Grotesk for headlines to echo the geometric confidence of the wordmark. Pair with generous whitespace and a single iridescent accent per composition.

Don't

Use decorative or serif typefaces for primary headlines. Crowd the layout with multiple gradients, competing colors, or excessive borders and dividers.
