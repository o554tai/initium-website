📐 Brand Usage Governance

Rules every agent and partner must follow
  • ✓
    Minimum Clear Space Logo must have padding equal to the height of the "I" in INITIUM on all sides. Never crowd with text or imagery.
  • ✓
    Color Lock Primary: #50C878 on white/light. Reverse: white on #1A1A1A. Never: gold, orange, gradient overlays, or color-shifted versions.
  • ✓
    No Distortion Never stretch, rotate, apply shadows, outlines, or effects to the logo. Maintain aspect ratio at all sizes.
  • ✓
    Typography Discipline Space Grotesk for headlines and labels. DM Sans for body. Playfair Display for editorial quotes only. Never substitute with system fonts.
  • ✓
    Tagline Pairing "Igniting Journeys" always appears below the wordmark, never above. Never alter the wording or translate without written approval.
  • Social Media Watermark All property photos, reels, and carousels posted by agents must include the INITIUM logo watermark or brand credit. Pending: batch watermark script.
  • Namecard Standard All business cards must use the approved template. No custom designs, no competing logos alongside INITIUM on the front face.
