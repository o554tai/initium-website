3. Logo Placement Rules

🎯 INITIUM Logo Lockup Requirements
  • INITIUM logo must appear at minimum 80% the height of the Partner logo
  • Minimum clear space: equal to the height of the "I" in INITIUM on all sides
  • Preferred arrangement: INITIUM logo positioned on the left or top
  • Both logos must be rendered in their original brand colors — no monochrome co-branding unless both parties agree in writing
  • No INITIUM logo on Partner materials without prior written approval for each use case

5. Attribution

All co-branded materials must include one of the following attributions:

  • "In partnership with INITIUM"
  • "Proudly co-presented by INITIUM"
  • "INITIUM is a registered trademark. Used under license."

6. Approval Process

All materials bearing the INITIUM logo must be submitted to legal@initium.sg for approval at least 5 business days prior to publication. INITIUM will respond with approval, requested changes, or rejection within 3 business days.

7. Term & Termination

This agreement is valid for the duration of the joint initiative or 12 months, whichever is shorter.

Either party may terminate with 14 days' written notice. Upon termination, the Partner must immediately cease all use of the INITIUM mark.

8. No Endorsement Implication

The Partner shall not state or imply that INITIUM endorses its products or services beyond the scope of this specific co-branding arrangement.
