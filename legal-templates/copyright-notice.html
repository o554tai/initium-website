3. Image Watermark Spec

All property photography, team photos, and branded graphics published by INITIUM agents must carry a watermark. Standard spec:

  • Text: "INITIUM" or "© INITIUM"
  • Position: Bottom-right corner, inset 2% from edges
  • Size: 4-6% of image width
  • Opacity: 50-70% on photos, 100% on graphics
  • Color: White on dark images, #50C878 on light images
  • Font: Space Grotesk, weight 600, uppercase
