Pre-Shoot Checklist
Shooting Script
Room-by-room capture

🚪 1. Reception / Entry

~2 min · 1 continuous take
  • Start at corridor facing the INITIUM logo / signage
  • Hold 3 seconds
  • Slow walk toward entrance door
  • Pan left-to-right covering both walls
  • Tilt down to floor mat, up to ceiling lights

🛋️ 2. Main Work Area

~3 min · 1 continuous take
  • Enter from reception, pause at doorway 3 sec
  • Walk perimeter clockwise — stay 1.5m from desks
  • Capture every agent's desk angle briefly
  • Pause at team photo wall / branding for 3 sec
  • End at window side with city view

📊 3. Meeting / Discussion Room

~2 min · 1 continuous take
  • Shoot from doorway looking in
  • Walk around table (either side)
  • Capture screen / projector wall
  • Pause at whiteboard with INITIUM branding

🪴 4. Pantry / Break Area

~1.5 min · 1 continuous take
  • Quick walkthrough — shows "human" side of office
  • Capture counter, fridge, seating nook
  • End with window light if available

🪟 5. Corridor / Window Gallery

~1.5 min · 1 continuous take
  • Walk the full corridor length slowly
  • Pan toward windows at 2–3 points
  • Show Great World City view if visible
Golden Rules
After the Shoot
1. AirDrop clips to your Mac or upload to Luma AI directly on 5G/WiFi.

2. Upload all 5 clips as one batch in Luma AI (it auto-stitches room transitions).

3. Wait 5–10 min for processing.

4. Export .splat file → SuperSplat cleanup → PlayCanvas embed.

5. Send Hermes the PlayCanvas link → he drops it into virtual-tours.html.
