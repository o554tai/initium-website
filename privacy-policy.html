Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 19, 2026

1. Who We Are

INITIUM is a registered real estate agency in Singapore. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or submit enquiries through our forms.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Property preferences, budget range, and unit type interests
  • Messages or notes you include in enquiry forms

We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our website:

  • IP address, browser type, and device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on site
  • Referral source and UTM parameters (e.g., which ad or campaign brought you here)

3. How We Use Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website (Google Analytics 4)
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns (Meta Pixel)
  • Retarget website visitors with relevant property listings on Facebook and Instagram

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Note that this may affect your experience on our site.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your property enquiries
  • Provide property recommendations and market updates
  • Send you relevant new launch information (only with your consent)
  • Improve our website and marketing efforts
  • Comply with legal obligations under Singapore law

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • INITIUM team members who assist with your property search
  • Service providers who help us operate our website and communications
  • Regulatory authorities when required by law (e.g., CEA, IRAS)

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or alteration. All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS.

7. Your Rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, contact us at tassocpc@gmail.com or WhatsApp +65 8846 4814.
